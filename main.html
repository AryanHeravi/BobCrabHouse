Our Story

From Iran to Jacksonville.

One man's journey from starting over in America to building a seafood business rooted in community.

Babak (Bob) started his business on the northside of Jacksonville with a vision of serving the community crab and seafood. Since 2009, Bob has been providing excellent customer service and sharing his knowledge of crab and seafood with every customer who walks in.

  • 1972Bob is born in Iran.
  • 2001He moves to America as a refugee.
  • ThenHe works multiple jobs until he finds his passion for crab and seafood.
  • 2009Bob opens Bob's Crab House.
